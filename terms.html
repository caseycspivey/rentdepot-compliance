SMS Terms of Service

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of service, employment, or completing any transaction with Rent Depot LLC. SMS notifications are an optional feature. You may access all Rent Depot services without consenting to receive SMS messages.

Service description

Rent Depot LLC operates an SMS notification service for work order assignment, scheduling, and status updates. The service is provided to Rent Depot maintenance staff and to residents of properties managed by Rent Depot.

How opt-in works

Enrollment in SMS notifications is always voluntary and is never pre-selected on your behalf:

  • Maintenance technicians opt in by checking an unchecked, clearly optional SMS consent checkbox on the technician onboarding form. The onboarding form can be submitted without checking that box.
  • Residents opt in by checking an unchecked, clearly optional SMS consent checkbox on the appointment booking confirmation page. The appointment can be confirmed without checking that box.

Permitted use

The SMS service is for work order assignment, scheduling, and appointment notifications only. It is not a marketing channel and is provided exclusively to Rent Depot maintenance staff and active residents requesting service.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Active technicians can expect 2–10 messages per day on work days. Residents typically receive a small number of messages associated with the lifecycle of an open work order or appointment.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your wireless carrier and plan. Rent Depot does not charge separately for SMS messages.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at pm@rentdepot.com or 844-736-8337. Opting out does not affect your access to services — work order and appointment information will continue to be communicated through other means.
