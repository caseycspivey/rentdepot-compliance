SMS Terms of Service

Service description

Rent Depot LLC operates an SMS notification service for work order assignment, scheduling, and status updates. The service is provided to Rent Depot maintenance staff and to residents of properties managed by Rent Depot.

Permitted use

The SMS service is for work order assignment notifications only. It is not a marketing channel and is provided exclusively to Rent Depot maintenance staff and active residents requesting service.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Active technicians can expect 2–10 messages per day on work days. Residents typically receive a small number of messages associated with the lifecycle of an open work order.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your wireless carrier and plan. Rent Depot does not charge separately for SMS messages.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at pm@rentdepot.com or 844-736-8337.
