What we collect

We collect mobile phone numbers from Rent Depot maintenance technicians and from residents who request maintenance service. We use these numbers solely to send SMS notifications about work order assignments, scheduling, appointments, and completion.

How we use phone numbers

  • Notify technicians when a work order is assigned, scheduled, or updated.
  • Send residents booking links and appointment confirmations and reminders.
  • Receive replies from technicians (e.g. "done", "need parts").

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. Phone numbers are used only for the operational SMS communication described above.

Opting out

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. Reply HELP for help. Opting out does not affect your access to Rent Depot services.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Frequency varies based on work order activity (typically 2–10 messages per day for active technicians).
